For dental labs and anyone who sells to a dental office · Twin Cities metro · federal provider records through Oct 11, 2026

32 new dental practices and 68 new dentists in the Twin Cities this year

A practice that just opened, or a dentist who just started, has no lab habit yet. These are the 102 that got their first NPI, the number every dentist needs before billing a patient, between Oct 12, 2025 and Oct 11, 2026. 93 work in general, prosthodontic or pediatric dentistry, the offices that send out dentures, partials and night guards.

    Source: the National Plan and Provider Enumeration System (NPPES), the federal register of every health provider's NPI, published by CMS and in the public domain. File: NPPES_Data_Dissemination_October_2026_V2.zip. Practices and dentists with a dental taxonomy (1223), a first NPI dated Oct 12, 2025 to Oct 11, 2026, a practice address in Anoka, Carver, Dakota, Hennepin, Ramsey, Scott or Washington County, and no deactivation. By county: Hennepin 48, Ramsey 22, Anoka 10, Washington 9, Dakota 7, Scott 3, Carver 3.

    A new NPI is the best public signal of a new practice or a dentist new to practice. It is not a licence date. Office build-outs come from the Minneapolis permit register, the only one in the metro that publishes, so they cover Minneapolis only. When no other dental provider shares a dentist's listed address, the address is left out, because it may be a home.
